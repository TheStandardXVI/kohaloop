Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

KohaLoop ("we", "us") is a community marketplace for selling, renting, and lending items between neighbours in New Zealand. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and neighbourhood, provided when you create an account.
  • Phone number (optional): if you choose to add one, shown to other users as a trust signal. Not verified by SMS.
  • Approximate location (optional): if you use "Use my current location" when posting an item, your location is fuzzed by roughly 300-500m before being stored, so your exact address is never shown publicly.
  • Listings and messages: the content of items you post and messages you send to other users through the app.
  • Photos: images you upload for your listings.

How we use this information

We use your information solely to operate the KohaLoop marketplace: displaying your listings, enabling messaging between neighbours, sending you email notifications when you receive a message, and processing optional paid features (boosting a listing, or unlocking an extra edit) via Stripe.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details — they are handled directly by Stripe in accordance with their own privacy policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your name, neighbourhood, and listings are visible to other users of the app, as this is necessary for the marketplace to function. Your email address is only shared with a user you choose to contact, and only to enable message notifications.

Data storage

Data is stored securely using Supabase (database and authentication) and Cloudflare (hosting). You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your choices

You can browse and search listings on KohaLoop without creating an account. An account is only required to contact sellers, post listings, or use favourites.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to request data deletion, please contact us through the app.
