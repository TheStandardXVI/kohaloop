Tool Rental in Queenstown, Straight From a Neighbour

Need a drill, ladder, or lawnmower for a weekend? No need to buy one you'll use twice a year.

Renting from a big hardware store for a one-off job adds up fast, and buying tools you'll rarely use just clutters the garage. On KohaLoop, locals across Queenstown and the wider South Island list tools and DIY equipment they're not using right now — for sale, for rent, or free to borrow.

What you can find

  • Power tools — drills, saws, sanders
  • Hand tools
  • Ladders and scaffolding
  • Garden tools — lawnmowers, hedge trimmers, and more

Why borrow from a neighbour?

It's usually cheaper than a rental store, and you're helping someone else get value out of a tool sitting idle in their garage instead of it going to waste. It's also often faster — no driving across town to a rental counter.
