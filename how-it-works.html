How KohaLoop Works

A free community marketplace to sell, rent, and lend items with neighbours across New Zealand's South Island.

1
Browse for free — search and filter listings by category, neighbourhood, or item status. No account needed to look around.
2
Create a free account — only needed when you want to message a seller, post your own item, or save favourites.
3
Choose how to list an item — for sale, for rent, or free to lend, whichever suits what you're sharing.
4
Message directly — arrange pickup, price, or rental dates one-on-one with your neighbour.

Why use KohaLoop instead of buying new?

Whether you're a seasonal worker in Queenstown who doesn't want to buy gear you'll only use for a few months, or a local wanting to make a bit of extra money from things gathering dust, KohaLoop keeps useful items moving around the community instead of sitting unused — or ending up in landfill.
