Ski & Snowboard Gear Rental in Queenstown, Straight From Locals

Skip the rental shop queue — borrow or rent gear from someone down the road on KohaLoop.

If you're spending a season (or just a week) in Queenstown or Wanaka, buying a full ski or snowboard setup rarely makes sense — and big rental chains can add up fast, especially for seasonal workers and backpackers who just need gear for a few months.

KohaLoop is a free community marketplace built for exactly this: real people in Queenstown, Wanaka, Arrowtown, Frankton and the wider South Island renting, lending, or selling their own ski and snowboard gear directly to neighbours — skis, snowboards, boots, jackets, helmets, and more.

Why rent from a neighbour instead of a shop?

  • Often cheaper than daily shop rental rates, especially for a full season
  • Gear is already broken in and sized by someone who actually used it locally
  • You're keeping money and gear circulating within the community instead of a rental chain
  • No inventory limits — real people list what they're not using right now

Browse listings with no account needed — filter by category, status (for sale, for rent, or free to borrow), and neighbourhood. Only need to sign up when you're ready to message someone or post your own gear. It's completely free to use.
